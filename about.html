I'm a UI/UX designer with a background in electrical and robotics engineering. I design mobile apps and websites, and I can build them in code.

Before design, I studied Electrical and Electronics Engineering, then completed an M.Tech in Robotics and Automation at the College of Engineering Trivandrum. For my master's thesis, I built an autonomous landmine detector using Raspberry Pi, Arduino, ROS and lidar.

Robotics taught me to think in systems: how the parts connect, where things break, and what happens next. I now bring that way of thinking to information architecture, interaction design and interface details.

I moved into UI/UX through Intellipaat's design certification and an internship at Zoople Technologies. Freelance mobile interface work gave me experience with user flows, wireframes and interactive prototypes. I also write HTML, CSS and Python.
