01

Overview

THE DESIGN INTENT

Make the next step obvious.

For RepUp, focus means giving the daily workout a clear starting point, then letting progress provide a reason to return.

RepUp brings guided workouts, personal plans and progress insights into one focused mobile experience. I worked from research and information architecture through a visual system and 23 high-fidelity screens.

02

Problem

Starting a workout shouldn't feel like work.

Beginners felt overwhelmed by choices and unclear guidance. More experienced users wanted meaningful progress data without being treated like beginners. Both needed a clearer route from opening the app to getting started.

127survey respondents
72%struggle with consistency
84%want a simpler experience
68%want visual progress

Project research: Google Forms survey (127 respondents) and eight interviews of 20–30 minutes. These are research findings, not measured product outcomes.

“Too many features crammed into one screen. I just want to start my workout without digging through menus and settings.”REPUP · INTERVIEW PARTICIPANT
03

Process

01

Listen & define

A survey, eight interviews and a review of Nike Training Club, Peloton and MyFitnessPal surfaced two needs: guidance for beginners and useful data for experienced users.

02

Map the daily loop

I organized the experience into six sections and three core journeys: onboarding, the daily workout loop, and upgrading to Pro.

03

Build & refine

Low-fidelity wireframes established hierarchy before the high-fidelity system. Peer and target-user feedback informed layout and interaction refinements.

04

Key decisions

DECISION / 01

Put the next workout first

Today's workout has its own start action on Home. The design target was a route of three taps or fewer, responding to the 55% who asked for quick-start workouts.

DECISION / 02

Make progress a reason to return

The weekly chart appears on Home, while Insights and Progress reveal longer-term trends. This responds to the 68% who asked for visual tracking.

DECISION / 03

Ask only what shapes the plan

Weight, height and fitness level form a short onboarding sequence. The goal was a fast, relevant start; the under-one-minute target still needs timed validation.
